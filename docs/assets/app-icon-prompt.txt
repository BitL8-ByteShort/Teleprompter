Original Teleprompter app icon, generated for this project.

A rounded graphite macOS icon with a mint camera lens above a reading panel.
Three high-contrast script lines and a mint cursor suggest reading near the
camera. Front view, restrained depth, no text or wordmark, no microphone or
play button, transparent outside the rounded tile. Clear at Dock icon sizes.

The 1024-pixel master is App/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/icon_512x512@2x.png.
Regenerate the smaller native sizes with script/generate_app_icon.sh using a
separate copy of that master as input. Xcode compiles the catalog into the app's
AppIcon.icns and Assets.car resources.
